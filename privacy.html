Privacy Policy (English)

Last updated: 13 October 2026

This policy applies to the website and services of ShopCloud ("we") and to our internal tools, including "VoxPix Uploader".

Information we collect

Name, contact details, industry, region, number of shops/staff and messages submitted through our enquiry form, used only to contact you and provide a quote.

Google / YouTube data

VoxPix Uploader is an internal tool used only with our own YouTube channels. It uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos we produce ourselves and to manage their metadata and thumbnails. We do not access, collect, store or share any other user's Google or YouTube data. VoxPix Uploader uses YouTube API Services; by using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.

Data retention and deletion

Enquiry data is kept for up to 12 months after handling and then deleted; you may request deletion at any time by email. VoxPix Uploader keeps only the video IDs of our own uploads locally for internal records. OAuth tokens are stored only on our own computer and can be revoked at any time via Google security settings; upon revocation we delete the stored token.

Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal data.
